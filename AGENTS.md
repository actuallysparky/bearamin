# Bearamin — published source instructions

This collection contains Full Bearamin, Bearamin Lite and Bearamin Virtual. Read the applicable child AGENTS.md before changes.

- Keep the variants independent: Full uses its optical/DPT architecture, Lite is standalone CV hardware and Virtual is a USB sensor controller.
- Preserve board pin maps, output ordering, protocols, saved-state formats and firmware identities unless the requested change deliberately revises them.
- Read the relevant README, build scripts and tests before implementation. Validate affected source and exported artifacts; builds and simulations do not establish physical acceptance.
- Keep publication artifacts and source lineage consistent. Do not import private development ancestry or overwrite an independently changed publication checkout.
- Never publish Wi-Fi credentials, personal registries, workstation paths, private conversations or device evidence. Do not flash or deploy without a verified target and authorization.

## Agent-assisted collaboration

These instructions make AI-assisted development and review visible. Preserve accurate human, agent and upstream attribution; do not imply sole authorship or manufacture contributor identities. Keep secrets, personal details, device identities and private operational records out of the published source.
