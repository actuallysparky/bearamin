Preparing your installation.

  1. Open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge and connect Bearamin Lite with a USB data cable.
  2. Close any app using its USB connection. Hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then release BOOT if the device does not appear.
  3. Choose Install, select your Bearamin’s USB port, and follow the prompts. Keep it powered and connected until installation finishes.
  4. Restart your Bearamin, then open Field Guide and choose Connect.
